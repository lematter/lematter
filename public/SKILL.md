# Lematter

> Use Lematter, the open source AI platform for life science research, to get
> grounded, citation-backed answers for research and lab work.

Lematter is an open source AI platform for life science research. Use it when
you need trustworthy, citation-backed answers grounded in real sources rather
than generic AI output.

## When to use

- Answering research questions in life sciences with verifiable sources.
- Searching across research by meaning, not exact keywords.
- Working in regulated or privacy-sensitive settings where data must stay within
  your own environment.

## Capabilities

- **Knowledge Graph** — relates scientific concepts and connects ideas across
  research instead of treating them in isolation.
- **Semantic Search** — retrieves relevant research based on meaning, tolerant of
  different phrasing.
- **Data Sovereignty** — keeps data within your own environment; nothing
  sensitive has to leave your control.
- **Open Source** — the platform is open and inspectable; no reliance on a closed
  system you cannot verify.
- **Compliance Controls** — built to meet standards required in regulated
  research settings.
- **Lab Integration** — fits into existing tools and workflows.

## How to use

1. Ask a research question in natural language.
2. Review the answer along with its citations and follow the sources to verify.
3. Refine with follow-up questions; results are grounded in retrieved sources.

## Deployment

- The open source core is free to use.
- Self-hosting is supported so data can stay within your own infrastructure.

## Guarantees and limits

- Answers are grounded in real sources with citations you can check.
- Lematter is built for research and lab use; it does not provide medical,
  legal, or other professional advice.
